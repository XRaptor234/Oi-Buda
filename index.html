Okay, now the sappy part.

💌

Oi Buda, my favorite human,

One whole year. A whole year of you and me being our own little kind of ridiculous.

I still think about how this started: a Tinder match, meeting at my place, and that little bracelet I made for you. Then that screenshot — you talking to your friend and calling me your girlfriend before we'd even had the official conversation. And me asking, “You like calling me your girlfriend?” You said yes, I told you to ask me, you did, and I said yes. I love that our story has this tiny, slightly chaotic prequel to the official beginning.

This year wasn't a perfectly edited montage. We had ups and downs, and there were things we had to learn along the way. I don't want to erase the real parts just to make the story look pretty. I want to remember what we learned, how we grew, and the moments that reminded us why this mattered.

Thank you for being part of my first year of this story. I hope the next year gives us more reasons to laugh until nothing makes sense, more memories to look back on, and more chances to love each other in ways that feel safe, honest, and real.

And if you ever come back to this page on a difficult day, let it remind you of one thing: you are loved, you are important to me, and our little story deserves care — not perfection.

Always your person (and certified menace),
me. ♡
